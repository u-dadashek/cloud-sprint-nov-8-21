## 🚨 THE GO/STOP TEST

Before spending ANY credits, ask:

1. **Urgency:** Do I need this in the next 7 days?
2. **Impact:** Will this make or save me $1000+?
3. **Uniqueness:** Can ONLY AI do this efficiently?
4. **Clarity:** Do I know exactly what I want?
5. **Readiness:** Are my inputs/data ready?

**ALL 5 = YES?** → GO, spend the credits
**ANY = NO?** → STOP, do it differently
