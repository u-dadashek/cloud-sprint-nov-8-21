## THE COGNITIVE STACK

**Core Insight:** "Intelligence doesn't persuade. Sequenced emotional engagement followed by logical validation persuades."

Every pitch must move through five cognitive layers in precise order:
1. **Intrigue** → Create "wait, what?" curiosity
2. **Credibility** → Establish authority without bragging
3. **Problem** → Make the pain visceral and costly
4. **Solution** → Show how you uniquely solve it
5. **Inevitability** → Prove why you'll win

Skip or reorder these layers and even the best idea fails.
